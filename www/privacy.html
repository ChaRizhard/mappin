MapPin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

MapPin is a trip planner. This page explains what information the app uses, where it goes and the choices you have. In short: your trips stay on your device unless you choose to sync or share them, and MapPin has no ads or tracking.

Information stored on your device

  • Your trips: stops, days, times, notes, costs, colours and groups.
  • Your settings, such as language, theme and map style.
  • Saved answers from map searches and opening hours, and map pieces you save for offline use, so the app works without a connection.

This information stays in the app's storage on your device. Deleting the app (or clearing your browser's site data) deletes it.

Your location

MapPin asks for your location only to show where you are on the map, to start the map near you (you can turn this off in Settings), and to measure distances in Today mode. Your location is used on your device and is not stored or sent to us. When you tap Directions, your starting point may be sent to Google Maps, which then handles the request.

Services MapPin connects to

To show maps and find places, the app sends map areas, search words and place coordinates (never your name or account) to:

  • Google: map images, and Google Maps when you open directions or reviews.
  • OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim, Photon, Overpass): place search and opening hours.
  • FOSSGIS OSRM: walking and driving routes.
  • Wikipedia, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons: place photos and short descriptions.

These services receive your device's internet address as part of any normal web request, and their own privacy policies apply.

Optional cloud sync

If you sign in to cloud sync, your email address and the trips you choose to save are stored with Supabase, our database provider, so you can open them on another device. Each person can only see their own trips. You can delete a cloud trip at any time from Settings → Cloud sync, and sign out to stop syncing. To delete your account, contact us (below).

Sharing

A share link contains the trip itself. Anyone with the link can open it, so share it only with people you trust. Short links (when cloud sync is on) store the shared trip with Supabase.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • MapPin isn't directed at children under 13.
